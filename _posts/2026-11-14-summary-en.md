### [Real-Time GPU-Accelerated Airband Radio Monitoring Tool Deployed for KORS](https://orcasairband.com/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

A developer released a real-time, GPU-accelerated airband radio monitoring tool built using an Ettus B210 software-defined radio and an NVIDIA dual-RTX 4090 hardware setup. The system employs a CUDA-based polyphase channelizer to capture the entire airband, demodulate signals, detect active transmissions, and discard idle data. The tool proved useful during an unexpected local ground stop affecting Orcas Island \(KORS\) and Victoria, Canada, by capturing local air traffic control chatter.

hackernews · mmaunder · Nov 13, 22:04 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50490593)

**「Background」** Software-defined radio \(SDR\) systems use configurable software to process radio frequency signals captured by specialized hardware tuners rather than relying solely on traditional hardwired analog components. Polyphase channelizers are digital signal processing structures commonly used in wideband receivers to efficiently split a broad frequency spectrum into multiple narrower, independent channels for simultaneous processing.

**「Impact」** Developers and aviation enthusiasts gain a high-performance reference architecture for processing wideband SDR spectrum data concurrently using GPU hardware acceleration.

**「Community Discussion」** Commenters discussed raw transcript excerpts captured by the system regarding the ground stop, while expressing surprise at how heavily garbled and difficult to understand standard analog airband voice transmissions can be to untrained listeners.
