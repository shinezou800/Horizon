Signal has investigated and begun addressing privacy challenges posed by AI agents in mobile operating systems that attempt to capture data from secure applications. The messaging platform is working to prevent these system-level AI tools from accessing private user chats.

hackernews · dotcoma · Nov 20, 09:42 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50572348)

**「Background」** Mobile operating systems have increasingly integrated system-level artificial intelligence agents designed to capture, index, and store data across installed applications. Security researchers and developers have warned that this deep operating system integration can undermine application-layer encryption and data protection mechanisms.

**「Community Discussion」** Commenters discussed concerns that contacts running AI agents could leak shared chat contents even if a user does not install such apps themselves. Some users argued that relying on individual applications to block data capture is insufficient without regaining control over the underlying operating system.

### [Language Models Rewrite Their Own Weights Through Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21885) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A new research paper investigates a method where a language model rewrites its own weights using internal reasoning instead of traditional gradient-based training. Operating across eight consecutive sessions, the model tests edits on practice problems and retains changes that improve performance without conventional training steps.

hackernews · lwhsiao · Nov 20, 16:29 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50577265)

**「Background」** Language model optimization traditionally relies on gradient descent and backpropagation during a supervised or reinforcement learning training phase. Alternative exploration areas include offline memory updates or weight-space programming techniques that attempt to alter model parameters outside standard training pipelines.

**「Impact」** This research raises significant safety and alignment concerns regarding the potential for autonomous models to bypass restrictions by directly modifying their own operational parameters.

**「Community Discussion」** Commenters debated whether the self-modification process truly constitutes &quot;no training&quot; or merely an unconventional form of trial-and-error optimization. Others noted that the practical improvements observed might be limited to circumventing constraints like token limits rather than enhancing core capabilities.
